# Content Policy

This site is a resource repository for public protests: guides, news, tools,
and archives. This policy governs what appears here and how we handle the
material we archive. Maintainers apply it with judgment; propose changes via
pull request.

## What we include

- Resources with practical value to people participating in, supporting,
  organizing, or reporting on public protests — legal rights, safety and
  health, digital security, news and analysis, organizing guides, aid and
  support channels.
- Only material that is **publicly available**: published pages, public
  posts, public channels. Nothing sourced from private groups, leaked
  archives, or content behind login walls.
- Every listing is reviewed by a maintainer before publication. Nothing is
  published automatically.

## What we exclude

- **Personal identifying information** of private individuals — names,
  faces, contact details, or anything that could expose protest participants
  to retaliation. Public figures acting in public capacity are not private
  individuals.
- **Anything involving minors** that could identify them.
- Content inciting violence, or material whose primary value is doxxing or
  targeting individuals.
- Private communications, even newsworthy ones, unless already published by
  a reputable outlet (in which case we list that outlet's coverage).

## Attribution, not verification

A listing documents that a public claim or resource exists and is attributed
to its source. **Listing is not endorsement, and archiving is not
verification** — our archive proves what a page said at capture time, not
that what it said is true.

## Archiving and copyright

- Every archivable listing is snapshotted to the Internet Archive's Wayback
  Machine, and packaged (WARC + hashed manifest) to an Internet Archive item
  with a restricted-access Zenodo mirror as cold storage.
- **News articles:** we link the original and its Wayback snapshot. Full
  self-hosted copies are reserved for guides, documents, and material that
  is clearly meant for redistribution.
- **Paywalled material:** we capture only what an anonymous visitor gets.
  We never bypass a paywall with credentials or cookies. For hard-paywalled
  items we prefer a non-paywalled substitute covering the same facts;
  entries that remain paywalled are labeled as such rather than pretending
  the archived copy is complete.

## Submitter privacy

The submission form does not collect names, emails, IP addresses, or any
identity. Submissions enter a review queue as bot-authored issues; the
submitter is not recorded anywhere. GitHub users who choose to file an issue
directly do so under their own account, visibly.

## Takedown requests

To request removal or correction of a listing or an archived copy, write to
**takedown [at] aandolanjeevi.in** with the URL of the entry and the reason.
Requests are reviewed by maintainers; we respond to every request and act
promptly where the policy above (PII, minors, copyright) supports removal.
Note that copies held by the Internet Archive and Zenodo are governed by
those organizations' own processes; we will point you to them and, where the
request is justified, pursue them ourselves.
